Thrivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Thrivo ("the app") helps you understand your activity, water, sleep and heart data. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Health data

With your permission, Thrivo reads data from Health Connect on your phone: steps, distance, active calories, floors, hydration, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, SpO₂, respiratory rate, weight, height, blood pressure, blood glucose, body temperature, VO₂max, exercise sessions and menstrual cycle data. It writes data you create in the app back to Health Connect: water you log, steps you add, weight, periods, and walks you record (including the route).

This data is processed only on your device to show charts and scores. Thrivo has no servers and no account system. We do not sell, share or use your health data for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties, except as described under "Optional features" when you turn them on.

Thrivo's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional features that use the internet

  • AI coach. Off by default. If you add your own Anthropic API key, your question and a summary of your recent numbers (steps, sleep, heart and recovery figures) are sent to Anthropic's API to generate an answer. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that request. Your key is stored only on your phone and is excluded from backups.
  • Best time to walk. If you allow approximate location, your location rounded to about 1 km is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service to get today's forecast.
  • Walk tracking. Uses precise location only while a walk is running; the route is saved to Health Connect on your phone.
  • Challenges. Share codes contain your name and this week's daily step counts and are only sent when you choose to share them.

Data stored by the app

Settings, goals, journal entries, shoes, caffeine and medication logs are stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, these settings (not your API key) are included in your own Google Drive backup.

Your choices

You can revoke any permission at any time in Health Connect or Android settings, and delete all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Data in Health Connect can be managed or deleted in the Health Connect app.

Children

Thrivo is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Thrivo is not a medical device. Scores and ranges are informational estimates and are not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice.
