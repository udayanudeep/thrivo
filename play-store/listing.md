## Full description (≤ 4000)
Thrivo turns the data your Fitbit already collects into a clear daily plan — steps, water, sleep and recovery in one calm place.

STEPS
• Day, week and month charts with your goal at a glance
• Step goal suggested from the Lancet 2022 daily-steps meta-analysis
• Streaks, badges, weekly challenges with friends, and GPS walk tracking
• Choose which app's steps count, so phone movement isn't counted as walking

WATER
• Log a drink in one tap, from the app, a widget or Quick Settings
• Goal based on EFSA guidance, with gentle reminders through the day

SLEEP & RECOVERY
• Sleep stages, sleep score, consistency (Sleep Regularity Index) and chronotype
• Recovery from your HRV and resting heart rate versus your own baseline
• Daily strain on a 0–21 scale, a strain target and tonight's sleep need
• Morning briefing and a wind-down reminder before your ideal bedtime

HEART & BODY
• Heart rate, resting HR, HRV, SpO₂ and breathing with normal ranges
• Cardio fitness (VO₂max), fitness age, training load and stress
• Weight, BMI, blood pressure, glucose and temperature from connected devices
• Cycle tracking, caffeine tracker, guided breathing and medication reminders

BUILT ON RESEARCH
Every score and range is tied to a published study or guideline (WHO, AHA, National Sleep Foundation, EFSA and more). Open "The Science" in the app to see all of them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your health data stays on your phone in Health Connect. No account, no ads, no tracking. The optional AI coach only runs if you add your own API key.

Requires Health Connect. Works with Fitbit and other apps that share data to Health Connect. Thrivo is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
